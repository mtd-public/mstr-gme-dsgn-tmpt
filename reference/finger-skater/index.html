SKATER

Weave the street. Don't bail.

  • Touch anywhere on the left to steer — the stick appears under your thumb
  • Hold anywhere on the right to charge an ollie, release to jump — longer hold, bigger air (and a little extra speed while held)
  • In the air, hold the right side again to grab the board for trick points — let go before you land or you eat it
  • Hit ramps (kicker, ramp, MEGA) for big air, hop obstacles and near-miss cars for bonus points
  • Three hits and you bail. Stay clean to build your multiplier
  • Land lined up on a rail to grind — steer left/right to keep your balance, tap the right side to pop off
  • Desktop: ←/→ to weave / balance, hold Space to charge, release to jump · C: camera
